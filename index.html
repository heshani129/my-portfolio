About Me

I am a Computer Science and Technology undergraduate at Uva Wellassa University of Sri Lanka, with a strong interest in software engineering, web development and machine learning. I enjoy turning ideas into working software, and I care about writing clean, readable code and building interfaces that are simple to use.

Through coursework and team projects, I have built a gym management system with React and an object-oriented PHP REST API, designed an agricultural marketplace for Sri Lankan farmers, and trained a Random Forest model to predict laptop prices in Python. These projects taught me how to work with databases, version control and teammates, and how to take a feature from planning through testing.

I am now looking for a software engineering internship where I can learn from experienced developers, contribute to real products and keep growing as an engineer.
